Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

Your video library

xplayer is an offline video player. It copies the video files and folders you choose into its own storage on your device. Video names, folder paths, and playlist order are saved locally so the app can organize and play your files. The app does not upload these files or library details to the developer.

Data collection

xplayer has no account system, advertising, tracking, analytics SDK, or developer-operated server. It does not collect data from the app for the developer. It does not access your contacts, location, microphone, or camera. It only accesses external files or folders you select through the system Files picker or explicitly open with the app.

File providers and backups

If you choose a file in iCloud Drive or another provider, that provider may download it before xplayer can copy it. Your device's backup settings may include xplayer's local files. File providers, Apple backups, and any system diagnostics you choose to share are governed by their providers' policies.

Keeping and deleting data

Your imported files and playlists remain on your device until you delete them or delete the app. Long-press a file or folder in Library to delete the local copy. Deleting a playlist removes its list of entries but keeps the videos. Deleting a local imported copy does not delete the file at its original source. Removing the app through iOS deletes its local app data; offloading an app may preserve data, according to iOS settings. Backups can have separate retention and deletion controls.

Support and links

Opening the support website or submitting a support request is optional. GitHub hosts the website and issue tracker and processes requests under its own privacy policy. A public support issue is visible to others. Do not include private videos, filenames, account credentials, or other sensitive information. The app never attaches your videos or library data to a support request.

If you choose to email a@ikel.ca, the maintainer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. That information is used to respond to your request and retained only as needed for support or applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by email.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised effective date. Contact the maintainer at a@ikel.ca or through https://github.com/ilovefood2/xplayer/issues.
