xplayer is a native offline video library for iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.

Import your own videos or folder trees from Files, organize them into folders, and create playlists that play in sequence.

No account. No ads. No tracking SDKs. Your videos and playlists are stored locally and are not uploaded to the developer.

Supports MP4, M4V, and MOV with the codecs available on your device. MKV, AVI, and protected/DRM content are not supported.

Read the user guide or read the privacy policy.
