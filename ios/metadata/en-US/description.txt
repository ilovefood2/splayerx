Keep your own videos organized and ready to watch with xplayer.

IMPORT YOUR LIBRARY
Choose videos or entire folders from Files, including supported file providers. Folder imports preserve nested folders and create local copies for offline playback.

ORGANIZE BY FOLDER
Create folders, move videos, and rename folders. You can also transfer files with Finder or the Files app.

BUILD YOUR PLAYLISTS
Combine videos from different folders into named playlists. Reorder or remove entries and play videos in sequence with previous and next controls.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, ads, or tracking SDKs. Your videos and playlists are stored locally and are not uploaded to the developer.

SUPPORTED FORMATS
MP4, M4V, and MOV containers using codecs supported by your device's Apple media player, typically H.264 or HEVC. MKV, AVI, DRM-protected media, and arbitrary desktop codecs are not supported. You provide your own video files; the app does not include a streaming catalog or download videos from websites.
